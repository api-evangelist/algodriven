# AlgoDriven

> AlgoDriven is an automotive AI company providing vehicle valuation, appraisal, inspection, and data-intelligence tools for car dealerships, banks, insurers, fleets, marketplaces, and OEMs. Its products include EvalExpert (car valuation), DriveExpert (test-drive/loan-car management), and InspectExpert (vehicle inspection), plus developer APIs for vehicle data, valuations, specifications, and vehicle history reports. Founded 2017; offices in Australia, UAE, and Egypt.
